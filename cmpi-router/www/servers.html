Network and upstream

Source: www/servers.html in the cmpi-router folder, installed by sudo ./bin/setup-services.sh servers. Dots: green = answers, red = should be up but is not, grey = off or not tested, amber = running but worth a look (booting, or a plain-text service is open). The web servers are tested from your browser (so a certificate problem shows as red); SSH, mail, FTP, telnet, the VAX, the terminal apps and mesh are tested from cpbee by status.php. The terminal apps ask for a user name and password. /servers/admin/ is this page behind a login, with buttons to start, stop and restart some of the services (sudo ./bin/setup-services.sh servers-admin installs it).
