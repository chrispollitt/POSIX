Facts worth remembering
  - Mailutils reads `#` in a netrc as a word, so the rendered ~/.netrc has no marker comment lines; the tickets
    file may keep them.
  - tvmail asks for imap://, so the vault record carries imap, imaps, smtp and smtps; each is rendered under
    cpbee.cmnet and bare cpbee.
  - The vault's secret key is backed up off-site (done by Chris, 2026-11-04).
